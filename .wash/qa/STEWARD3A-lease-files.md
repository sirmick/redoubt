> 
> Wall 1, admission: walfsd (and littlefsd) admit in_flight: 2 multiplexed sessions per bucket, keyed by account (serving.md "Buckets"). > 
> Options: (1a) in_flight 6 in walfsd/littlefsd, BUDGET/heap raised, pages updated; (1b) key leases apart: against serving.md.
