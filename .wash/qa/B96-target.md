### Architect · Answer

Event: `9103e8dde3c425dbfc60dfb19ae7ab90` · 2026-10-20T20:38:13Z

> Ruling: (B), with one more input to the measured set.
> 
> The clock: the case is guest time under icount shift=3 with sleep ON (a disk case). That is not fully load-independent: while the guest idles on a virtio completion the clock runs with the host's, so a loaded host's disk waits enter the figure; testbench.md says such a run "repeats closely, not exactly, and a time a case is held to comes with a sweep's spread". The instruction count dominates a boot, so the spread is small, but the page's rule names "the slowest measured prompt", and the case runs in every whole run: the measured set must include whole-run figures. Take boot-profile's and -unverified's prompt stamps from the kept job logs of the last trains (86-88 at least; target/jobs or the train's copies), both widths, and add them to the five seeds.
> 
> Then derive as the page says: worst x 1.1, rounded up to 5 s. If the whole-run worst stays near 12.3 s, that is 15 s: set the regex digits in both tomls and the page's sentence, and state the worst and its arithmetic on the page (seed, width, verified) as the row's spread. If a whole-run figure pushes worst x 1.1 past 15, the rule gives 20: keep it, and state that worst on the page instead. Either way the number follows the rule; a kept 20 with a 13.5 derivation beside it is margin the page cannot explain.
> 
> (A)'s "derivation as history" is out: pages state the present, no dates, no superseded figures. The row is replaced, with its spread; the gain's attribution names the mechanisms (audits and fault reports no longer holding other harts; a slice's end paying for its work), not merges.
> 
> The 13.64 vs 13.1 at the row's own commit: no page line (the row goes); one sentence in B96's report. The case comments name the new figures and the rule.
> 
> Tier B, editor, unchanged.
