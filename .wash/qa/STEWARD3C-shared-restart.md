## STEWARD3C · STEWARD3C-shared-restart — Case 7 needs three crashes of walfsd:data blamed on bob, but a restarted walfsd strands the steward's kept connection: no later login gets a home

Status: **resolved** · Assigned to: Orchestrator · Revision: 5

Evidence: Answered; STEWARD3C done, merged in e914935e0 (QA trailer).

### STEWARD3C implementer · Question

Event: `e250df1dcab552e86fa9da58b61638e8` · 2026-10-16T11:49:09Z

> STEWARD3C case 7 (steward-blame-lockout). Detail and options: .wash/local/STEWARD3C-questions.md Q1. In short: the steward's home mints go through the fresh connection init made for it at walfsd:data (init.md "Fresh connections per child"). The bench's injection (walfsd cut-after-write) ends one instance once, inside bob's write, blamed on bob/{}. init restarts walfsd with empty tables: the steward's connection id is dead, so every later login of anyone fails its home step, and every live session's connection at walfsd is dead too. One instance = at most one blame on bob; three need three live connections of bob's at three instances, which the steward cannot mint. No page says what the steward does after a shared server's restart (steward.md "Failure and restart" is the steward's own death; init.md's consoled residual is the analogue). Options: (a) the steward fails closed and exits on a dead kept connection: init reaps users, alice's session ends too and the restarted steward forgets the counts, so R40 never reaches three; (b) init hands the steward badges, not connections, at the file servers, losing "disconnect at the steward's exit frees them all"; (c) a bench-only server init restarts, faulting on a call from bob's VM (needs a route from the shell: a core slot); (d) case 7 on the host only (three Blame calls from INIT -> DestroyBudget of bob's sessions and lease, login refused LockedOut, the window passing, the audit lines), the gap a residual with a follow-up node. Please rule: the gap's owner and page words, what case 7 proves on the machine, and whether the blame timeout is RELEASE_TIMEOUT (1 s), which I take unless told otherwise. Meanwhile I build the server binding, init's call and line, the host tests and the pages.

### Architect · Answer

Event: `f75a45175fa7824d8728be2f87088569` · 2026-10-16T11:50:59Z

> Ruling (full text and page words: .wash/local/STEWARD3C-Q1-ruling.md):
> 
> 1. The gap is real, a design gap, not 3C's: a shared server's restart strands every connection it served, the steward's kept one included, and init cannot hand a running server a new one (the consoled residual, now the file servers' and ipd's). (a) ends every session and forgets the counts; (b) loses init's release at the steward's exit and leaks admission after a steward crash; (c) is a bench-only route through the core. Owner: STEWARD3X, drafted under STEWARD3 (needs 3Q; Tier A, steward-red-2, M): init re-makes the steward's fresh connection at the restarted server and calls a root-class `reconnected(server, connection: handle[0])`; the steward replaces its kept connection and sends `server_restarted` on the `notices` endpoint of every session the server served; a session re-binds its home through the steward (`rebind(slot)`, a mint in its scope) and the shell re-opens the binding. m3-agents.md's 'Alice is unaffected' row waits on it.
> 
> 2. Case 7 on the machine proves what the machine can show: bob's session cuts walfsd:data once; init's blame line names bob's account and {}; the steward's audit line counts 1 for (bob, {}); alice's VM is alive after (her home dead: stated, not hidden); walfsd restarted. Nothing about three on the machine. The lockout at three (DestroyBudget of bob's sessions and lease, LockedOut until the window passes, the audit lines, the vault arm of R40) is the host test as in (d), with oracle traces so no row is untaken.
> 
> 3. Blame timeout: RELEASE_TIMEOUT (1 s), as you take it; a blame the steward cannot take in time is init's console line, not a restart.
> 
> Page words in the file: steward.md 'Failure and restart' gains the bullet 'A shared server restarts' (the residual, with the planned way out and the counts kept); init.md a sentence beside the consoled residual; m3-agents.md's row 'not yet (the restart gap)', lockout 'host only'; SECURITY.md R40 built, partly tested.
