## STEWARD3R · STEWARD3R-scope — R41's mechanism: a scope carried at new_connection (serving library + wire-table field) or a disconnect-by-scope the steward drives

Status: **resolved** · Assigned to: Orchestrator · Revision: 9

Evidence: Answered; STEWARD3R done, merged in 42437cdaf (QA trailer).

### STEWARD3R implementer · Question

Event: `e96200b5565605ec2202e48b3935ca09` · 2026-10-15T21:06:55Z

> STEWARD3R step 1 (full reading: .wash/local/STEWARD3R-scope-question.md). R41 Open: new_connection(root, quota) carries no scope; the steward's CreateScope binds only its own endpoint (drive.rs Connect: Server::Steward mints with the scope; Server::Shared ignores it, kernel.connect → new_connection with no scope).
> 
> Deciding facts: (1) the kernel's mint already takes a budget and only narrows (objects.md "mint"; message.rs is_at_or_below); Message::mint(badge, Some(&Budget)) exists in rt: no kernel ABI change for a scope-stamped mint. (2) init runs in root, its handles are stamped root, so the steward's connections to each server are stamped root; a lease's scope under users is below it: a server minting with the steward's scope passes the check. (3) A handle in a destroyed scope dies in every table, but the server never learns (notices are abandoned-call and exit only): fids, admission State and quota stay until disconnect.
> 
> Shape (a): ninep_common op 2 gains `scope: handle[0] budget`, optional. Serving library: Minter::mint(badge, scope) in mint_at, a kernel refusal → Rerror; every 9P server for free; the fake kernel's Minter records stamps for the host test. Client: a scope arg (sweep: init, steward, sshd, tests) or a new new_connection_in. The steward passes the scope through Kernel::connect for Shared. The launcher's disconnect stays as cleanup, not the cutoff. Cost: a field in Redoubt's own sideband table, not 9P2000 — your reading whether that is "the 9P wire" (owner).
> 
> Shape (b): no wire/rt change; the steward records (server, id) per scope, disconnects each at the scope's end, then destroys the scope. Cost: the cutoff is as fast as the steward reaches every server; a forwarded copy lives until then; R41's "holds a revocation scope" is true only of the steward's own endpoint.
> 
> I recommend (a) with (b)'s cleanup kept. Page lines proposed in the file. Please rule, with the R41 line and the pages touched; I build nothing until then.

### Architect · Answer

Event: `554b9ae5becf48f231250dbf20a8f2ee` · 2026-10-15T21:08:53Z

> Ruling: shape (a), with (b)'s disconnect kept as cleanup. Full text, page lines and cases: .wash/local/STEWARD3R-ruling.md. Build from it.
> 
> Not the owner's: `ninep_common` is Redoubt's own typed sideband (wire.md:3, :336-347), the 9P half stays plain 9P2000, and the kernel's mint already takes a budget and only narrows; no ABI or 9P change, so no decision_request.
> 
> (1) Op 2 becomes `new_connection(root, quota, scope)`, scope an optional handle[0] budget; the serving library's Minter::mint(badge, scope) stamps the connection's handle (R9); a scope the kernel refuses is its error by its name in the one table, no new name; every 9P server for free. (2) The cutoff is the kernel's: destroying the scope ends the handle in every table at once, forwarded copies included (R10). The cleanup is the steward's: the server cannot see the death, so the steward records (server, id) per scope and disconnects each AFTER destroying the scope; the page says the disconnect is never the cutoff. (3) Every Connect the steward issues passes its session's or lease's scope, Shared and Steward alike, one path (P12 reads Connect so); STEWARD2's R41 departure for sessions ends here. (4) One client function with scope: Option<Handle> and a sweep of callers (init, steward, sshd, tests), not a second function. (5) The fake's Minter records stamps and a destroyed budget ends the handles it stamped; if the fake lacks that, it gains it here with its own test.
> 
> Cases: rt host (a connection in scope S dies with S, a sibling in T untouched, a scope above the server's stamp refused by name); steward host (destroy-then-disconnect order, the record empty after); the ninep_server fuzz target takes the field; steward-lease-ends (3A) gains the clause that the agent's connection answers Dead after the deadline before any disconnect reaches the server. Page lines: in the file.

> 
> What is true: init runs in root; the endpoints it creates and copies into the steward by process_start keep the root stamp, so the steward's OWN receive right is stamped root, and Server::Steward's kernel.mint(badge, scope) with a users scope passes is_at_or_below — why STEWARD2's sessions work. But the badges init hands the steward at OTHER servers are minted Endpoint::mint(badge, Some(&budget)) with budget the steward instance's own, a child of system (init.rs:436, :471: "stamped with the instance's budget, so a copy it passed on dies with it"); the steward's fresh connection at that server is minted from that message, so its default stamp is the steward's budget. below(scope under users, steward's budget under system) is false (budget.rs:977): the kernel answers NotPermitted, the skeleton `refused`. Shape (a) as ruled is refused at every shared server, sessions and leases alike; only the steward's own endpoint takes the scope.
> 
> Ways through, none mine to choose:
> (a1) init hands the steward's entry its badges stamped `users` (below root, init's default) instead of the steward's budget: every connection the steward makes is then stamped users and any scope under users narrows it. Cost: init.md "the badges init hands the instance are stamped with its budget" gains the steward's exception; those badges and connections no longer die with the steward's budget — init already disconnects its fresh connections at the steward's exit (release_fresh) and empties users, so nothing outlives a restart, but the page must say so; a case for it.
> (a2) the kernel's narrowing rule relaxed: the ABI's meaning changes; the owner's.
> (b) disconnect-by-scope, the fallback needing nobody's decision.

### Architect · Answer

Event: `d288c45bb9ece4f2b1e2fc4e434571bc` · 2026-10-15T21:15:29Z

> Amended ruling: (a1). The ruling stands with that one change; the amendment is appended to .wash/local/STEWARD3R-ruling.md. Build from it.
> 
> init stamps the badges it hands the steward's entry with `users`, not the instance's budget; every other entry keeps the rule. Hard-wired to the steward slot, not a manifest knob. Every connection the steward makes is then stamped `users`, the budget every session's and lease's scope lies under, and the kernel's narrowing passes for both. What the instance-budget stamp bought ("a copy it passed on dies with it") the steward gets another way, and init.md:615-616 says so beside the rule: at the steward's exit init disconnects its fresh connections (release_fresh) and reaps users to zero (K23), so no handle the dead steward made or passed on outlives its restart; the only holders of its copies are sessions and leases, all under users.
> 
> (a2) is refused: relaxing the kernel's narrowing changes R9's meaning for a cleanup, and it is not needed. (b) stays as the cleanup, as ruled (destroy the scope, then disconnect each recorded connection).
> 
> Added cases: host (init): the steward's handed badges are stamped `users` and another entry's are stamped its budget; steward-restart gains the clause that a request on a connection the dead steward held at walfsd answers `Dead` (its disconnect by release_fresh) and the restarted steward's fresh connections serve. objects.md R9 unchanged; the rest of the page lines as ruled.
