## B94 · B94-cause — lease-writes' idle session reset: one lost keep-alive answer is fatal

Status: **open** · Assigned to: Architect · Revision: 9

### Implementer · Question

Event: `2336ac7c77448316fae22137ef10b498` · 2026-10-20T21:00:11Z

> B94 mechanism (detail: .wash/local/B94-identification.md). ipd sets set_timeout(60 s) + set_keep_alive(30 s) on every established socket (stack.rs). In smoltcp 0.14 the timeout runs 60 s from the last segment IN, the keep-alive 30 s from the last segment OUT, and dispatch checks the timeout before it sends the probe. An idle session (lease-writes waits minutes for lease ends) lives on one exchange per 30 s: one lost ACK to ipd's probe, or no ipd turn between T+30 and T+60, resets it at T+60. Zero tolerance. A loss path exists: netd drops a frame ipd has not taken within 50 ms (netd.md SEND_TIMEOUT_US), and nothing retransmits a keep-alive's answer. Probe build (ipd timeout/late-wake lines, netd drop lines, card pcap, host ss watchdog): solo PASS 596 s, 2/2 PASS beside an 8-case side load (it did not contend the case's core); every idle gap closed by probe+ACK at 30.0 s, slirp answering in <1 ms. Not reproduced; rate 1 in 1 whole bench (train 86). An exclusive cases-rv64 run is queued.
> 
> 1. Can a fix on the mechanism be ruled without a reproduction? (a) keep-alive at a fraction of the timeout, e.g. 10 s against 60 s: 5 probes, 4 losses tolerated. That is a timer change, so I'm asking first. (b) keep the timers, but give a socket with nothing unacknowledged no smoltcp timeout, and end it in ipd after N unanswered probes (smoltcp keeps no probe count). (c) no fix: a residual on ipd.md (an idle socket survives no lost answer) beside netd's drop, the case's miss a finding.
> 2. What must the exclusive run show? On a failure: the component, from the pcap and probe lines (netd dropped the ACK; ipd woke ≥30 s late; the ACK never reached the card, i.e. slirp/host; or a FIN from sshd, not ipd's RST). On a pass: 0 in 1, with the run's worst ipd lateness and its netd drops as the measured margin.
> Either fix is in servers/ipd (Tier A; B97 queued behind).
