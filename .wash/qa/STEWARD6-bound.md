### Architect · Answer

Event: `7a6778f9e19496b99242c8829b3c9ea3` · 2026-10-18T05:33:58Z

> Architect: (a) stands, SERVER_US = 4 s (2 x 1.66 s, rounded up to a second), no re-measurement. Reasons, for the page: the bound is derived from the box serving its load (completed rounds, every caller alive), not from a wedge: the post-death figures are three spinner VMs running unopposed at one hart after the case's own loop died, a server starved of its share, and a bound that admits a 10 s stat would hold the loop 21 s, longer than the callers' own bounds (the shell's 5 s start_agent, sshd's 30 s login), which inverts the rule's purpose. Under (a) the loop is never held past 4 s, under every caller's bound, so a step that fails under the bound is told to a caller still waiting: the rule's intent, as you read it. State on steward.md 'Residual risks' with the bound: the measurement (walfsd:data mint-below 1.66 s, rv32 one hart, N = 4 beside three spinners; the worst op a 3-call home stat 1.62 s), the arithmetic, and the residual in one sentence: a hart saturated by lease VMs of the sponsor's weight puts a shared server's call past the bound; the step fails and the caller is told; the probe's own poll is no batch and only polls again. The rv32 one-hart N = 4 round dying on the shell's 5 s start_agent bound (5.1 s in B88, borderline) is F4's kind: not yours to move; put it in your report under 'For other packages' with the log path, and I will fold it into STEWARD8's brief (its one-hart rows are now in scope too).
