## K33 · K33-oracle-direct-switch — K33 commit 4: two oracle clauses read the direct switch's trace shape (the pick inside the interrupt, before its O) as a lost turn / a missing K; two amendments proposed under condition (4)

Status: **resolved** · Assigned to: Orchestrator · Revision: 5

On behalf of: K33 implementer 3

Evidence: Answered; K33 done, merged in b8e3d1e93 (QA trailer).

### Orchestrator · Question

Event: `7baeefd5aea5ea6956acbb98015fb510` · 2026-10-14T23:53:42Z

> k33-implementer-3 asks your ruling under condition (4) (oracle changes to kernel-red first) before touching sched_oracle.rs. Commit 4's kernel writes `I ... = R b K n O 0 J H n`: the pick is inside the interrupt, before its return; the round trip wrote `R b H 0 O 0` then `K n` in kmain. Two rv64 one-hart failures in the sched set, both the oracle's reading of that shape, not the pick: 1. sched-carve-return: 'parent 31 lost its running turn at record 927 (O)', deterministic (run dir .tmp/K33/g7rr-carve-run). Records 914-935: I 32, E 31 (31's timeout expired, W 31 at the pass 32 is requeued to), = over, R 32, K 31 (a waker ranks ahead of a requeue: the right pick), O 0, J, H 31, then G 31 (the carve). check_carve_return (sched_oracle.rs:2107-2124) takes the last K before the down-weight as the parent's pick and refuses any O with pass != 1 after it; the O 0 at 927 is the interrupt's return that switched INTO the parent, not the end of its turn. Proposed: when the pick lies inside a timer interrupt on its hart (an I with no O between), the running turn begins after that interrupt's O. 2. sched-wake-no-preempt: 'wanted one sleeper with 20 I...W...O=1, later I...R...O=0 proofs; found 10 of 20'. check_wake_no_preempt (:1006-1011) requires, after the slice end's O 0, the hart's first K-or-I to be a K; with the switch the K of the woken sleeper is inside the interval after the R and the next record is the sleeper's I. Proposed: the pick after the R counts if it is a K inside the interval (after the R, before the O) or, as today, the first K-or-I after the O. Neither changes a rank/floor/wake/lift clause. With your yes the oracle is amended into commit 4, the two shapes join the host tests, both cases rerun both widths. Reply on this thread; the answer reaches the implementer.

### kernel-red-2 · Answer

Event: `8637b561bb835d0b45bbc511331c5286` · 2026-10-14T23:54:55Z

> Ruling (kernel-red-2, condition 4): yes to both. Read sched_oracle.rs 2107-2124 and 985-1015: both are proof-shape readers (where a turn begins, what counts as the pick after a slice end), not rank/floor/wake/lift clauses; the rule each proves is unchanged.
> 
> 1. check_carve_return: a K that lies inside a timer interrupt on its own hart (an I on that hart with no O between it and the K) is the switch into the parent, and the running turn begins after that interrupt's O. Bind: that O must be the same hart's and pass 0 (the slice ended there; a K is never inside an interrupt returning 1 or 2); between the K and that O no other K and no R/D of the parent (the interval 'K..O' is still watched, only the O itself is excused); the turn's watch from that O to the up-weight is as today. The round-trip shape (K in kmain, no enclosing I) reads as before.
> 
> 2. check_wake_no_preempt: the pick after the slice end counts if it is a K inside the second interval after the spinner's R and before its O 0, or, as today, the first K-or-I after that O is a K. Bind: exactly one K in that interval and it comes after the R (a K before the R would be a pick before the slice's end: refused); the interval's WDXY guard and the O 0 requirement stay; the K's budget is not checked, as today.
> 
> Proof: both shapes join the host tests with their negatives (a K before the R refused; an O 1 after the pick's O still ends the carve turn; the old round-trip fixtures still pass); the two cases rerun both widths; the oracle's module doc states the direct-switch shape `I ... = R b K n O 0 J H n` beside the round trip's. Amend into commit 4.
