## What IDLE2 found and did (reasoning not in the report)
- Cause at rest: `time::rearm` armed every hart for the earliest timeout/deadline; SCHED4 placed an item on its payer's hart but left a payer-nowhere item (every blocked one-thread budget) every hart's. At rest that is every item, so each periodic sleep fired on all four harts; three took the lock for nothing, each halted on it and was woken by the releaser's IPI; each re-armed because the expiry moved the hint. Measured before (train 25 main, 60 s, 4 harts): rv64 371 acquire_ticket wakes, 345 set_timer vs 197 s_timer, 412 wake_halted IPIs, busiest hart 3.5/s; rv32 313/319/173/353, 3.4/s.
- Rule (commit 1, libs/stride Hint::due_on): a payer-nowhere or payer-None item is armed on a hart unless ANY other hart is armed at or before it (armed[g] <= at; 0 = fired and re-arming counts as covering); the payer's own hart always arms; with the payer running somewhere only its harts cover (SCHED4 unchanged). So the first hart to re-arm after the note owns the item: usually the hart the thread left (it re-arms at that return/idle), but path-dependent; the rule bounds the COUNT of harts woken (one), not which. Liveness = SCHED4's argument: every exit re-arms under the lock (harts decide in turn, never two at once: the stride fixpoint test re-arms sequentially; the simultaneous form oscillates); a covering hart keeps the item or sees another covering; one covering only by its own slice end re-arms at that end and arms the item. One hart: unchanged. rearm's sites (sched::leave at the return to user mode after the switch, kmain's idle before wfi, on_interrupt(from_user=false) in the idle), note_* (record only), due() (global hints) and the model: UNCHANGED by IDLE2 — binding 5, confirmed in every commit.

## SCHED4 facts an IDLE2 implementer must know
- Hint { at, payer: Option<u64> } in libs/stride/src/lib.rs ~1160-1205; Hint::lowered (earlier replaces; equal with another payer -> None; later ignored); Hint::due_on(h, runners, armed) is THE placement rule; time.rs rearm (kernel/src/time.rs ~104-119) calls it per kind with sched::runner_ids() and t.armed; feature timer-any-hart = the plain `at` (the recorded negative). armed[h] = 0 at every timer interrupt (on_interrupt) and at expire_due's fire; due_on treats a <= at as covering, so 0 covers ("fired, re-arming at its return").
- Payer of a timeout = the blocked thread's budget (message.rs settle); of a deadline = the dying budget's parent (budget.rs ~947). A one-thread budget's timeout: payer runs nowhere while it waits.
- SCHED4's gate: foreign_timer_in=<share>:<per mille> on the host-clock twins deadline-placement, deadline-flood-billed-traced-mttcg, sched-exit-churn-mttcg at 10; icount cases record (turn order). All re-run green with IDLE2's rule (0-4 per mille).
- K33: a slice end picks and switches inside the interrupt; both paths return through sched::leave(pid != 1) which rearms after the switch; kmain's idle rearms before wfi; leave(KERNEL_PID) does not rearm.
- Stride host tests (libs/stride/src/tests.rs ~848-980): armed_at re-arms harts in turn to a fixpoint; the placement test asserts payer-nowhere/None items on at most one hart (exactly one when nothing earlier covers; at one hart that hart); transient tests for the switch-away and the covering hart's slice end.
