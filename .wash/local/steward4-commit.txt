steward: declassification and push through a crossing budget, the confined deployment's way in, and the responsiveness targets measured with the real steward

An item leaves a label set only by a granted approval, read through a crossing budget carrying
exactly the item's labels and copied out as the approved snapshot, never re-read (R42); input
enters a confined domain only by a push the label's owner submits from an unlabelled session and
approves at approve@box.

The crossing: `servers/crossing`, one program with a mode (`crossing read|write`, wire table
libs/wire/tables/crossing.md, opcodes 1-2), launched by the steward into a `sizes.crossing`
budget (128 pages: the steward's sixteen-page lend maps into it beside the stub and the image)
stamped with the labelled side's account, with one connection to the vault's walfsd minted in
the crossing budget's scope (R41) at the vault's root (a mint below the root walks with the
caller's labels, which the unlabelled steward has not: the labelled program walks to the item),
bounded by CROSSING_LIFE; it serves one call and exits, or is killed with the budget. The
steward's drive runs the core's Read and Write steps and nothing else; the request is frozen on
the snapshot's hash; a declassified item lands at ~/out/<name> in the submitter's unlabelled home
(an existing file is `in_use`, never overwritten); a push's source is read by the unlabelled
steward itself, snapshotted, hashed and shown on the screen by size and digest, bounded by
PUSH_MAX (61,440 bytes: fifteen lend pages, the sixteenth for the path and lengths), `too_big` by
a stat before the core; a writer that fails after some bytes leaves the vault's short file, the
next push overwrites it. The steward's heap is 128 pages in the image's and the steward
manifests (PENDING_CAP pushes' snapshots beside a read's buffer; 32 failed one 4 KiB push's read
on rv64). The steward says `served submit|end_lease in N us` on each (steward.md's residual:
submit at most 100 ms rv64 / 95 ms rv32, a VM lease's end at most 589 ms on rv64 at one hart).

Confined deployments: init's confinement check counts a home's volume for the principal's
unlabelled domain alone, since the steward binds a home to unlabelled sessions only under
`confined` (one of init's own lines to the steward); a labelled session is told no home and the
shell says so in one line; an exempt server (the steward, sshd) handed an endpoint is not counted
as its user, the session it mints for is, the server's R25 check and the steward's binding rule
holding independently of init's count (init.md's sentence); alice's net leaves the confined
manifest. steward-confined-push boots a two-disk confined manifest on B79's disk2 (the vault on
its own labelled blkd): the vault session starts at `/`, the push lands, the vault reads it back.

The targets with the real steward: steward-latency (a test-only `latency-probe` in the
steward's binary, built by that case alone: a bench host test holds it to that) measures the
steward's timer wake, decision wake and budget_destroy call, the kernel's deadline notice and
R10 from the trace, net of the checked build's audits, at N = 1 and 2 live leases of alice's
(the probe's or the deadline's among them) on both widths at one hart and two; the probe arms
its timer at the last point before the receive that waits on it and prints the reply-to-receive
gap as `reply_wait`; the window ends at `LATENCY-END` after the second round's counts, which the
oracle takes as its edge (nothing after it judged), the rounds closed by marker files the
session writes; the case ends by the steward's marker-file exits and init's reboot, which dumps
the ring (2.1-2.9 million records, none dropped). Findings, not tunings: N = 4 and 16 are not
measured (when the case was written the box admitted eight steward-minted system-volume
connections, the steward's own share at erofsd:system; a minted connection is now admitted in
its account from its first request, so those rounds await a run); N = 3 is not reached (a cleanup step dropped at a busy server leaks a
State; the cleanup step's parking is the follow-up); the timer wake's p50 misses at every width
and hart count and, at two harts, the decision wake's p50 and the deadline notice's p99, the
steward reaching its wait with its reply's console round trips on its pass (the loop's console
writes are the follow-up). The kernel-deadline leases are 5 s: a 2 s lease died inside its own
launch at two harts (B82). Every other target is met; the table and the sentences are on
scheduling.md "Responsiveness". The case is out of the whole run (`whole_run = false`), run by
name, until the loop's console writes are fixed: a whole bench is a verdict on the tree.
