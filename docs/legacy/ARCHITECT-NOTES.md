# Design orientation

- [Documentation map](README.md): the owner of each contract; TENETS has precedence.
- [Open decisions](QUESTIONS.md): unresolved IDs and the next unused ID.
- [Approval provenance](ANSWERS.md): accepted decisions and prior amendments.
- [Current implementation](STATUS.md): built behavior and limitations.
- [Package claims](SWARM.md#claims): integration state; [BUILD-PLAN](BUILD-PLAN.md) owns remaining acceptance.

Keep rules and their rationale in the owning specification. Do not maintain another decision
or progress log here. Changing a wire table requires the generator drift check.
